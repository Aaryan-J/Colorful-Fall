# Colorful Fall

Autumn seems rather bland this year. Everything has lost its color. You, as a kitty, need to save fall!

Type away and return color to the world again, because no one can take a peaceful nap in such a condition!

## Tutorial

- Click on the kitty for in-game tutorial
- Each region is greyed out, click on the region to bring down the typing prompt!
- Each correct word gets you progress towards making the world colorful again... and CATNIP!
- Spend catnip in the shop (bottom right region) to buy upgrades that will help you on your journey!
Color the world in, little cat.
